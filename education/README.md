# Education

Trust flows for the education domain: issuance of a Maturitätszeugnis (upper-secondary
school leaving certificate) and university onboarding using the e-ID and Maturitätszeugnis VC.

> Not an official flow of the swiyu team or any other authority — published without
> warranty. Work in progress, for discussion purposes only.

## Matura Credential Issuance Without the e-ID

DIDAS proposal for discussion, extending the High School Certificate Issuance
flow: the school identifies the student with the school account, not the e-ID,
and a governing authority keeps the list of schools authorised to issue the
credential. Use case:
[#121 Authenticate a Matura qualification for admission](https://github.com/DIDAS-swiss/use-case-repository/issues/121).
