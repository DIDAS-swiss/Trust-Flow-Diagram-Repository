# Matura Credential Without the e-ID

DIDAS proposal for discussion. The school identifies the student with the school
account, not the e-ID. A governing authority sets the rules for the Matura
credential and keeps the list of schools authorised to issue it. The university
later checks the credential at admission.

> Not an official flow of the swiyu team or any other authority — published without
> warranty. Work in progress, for discussion purposes only.
