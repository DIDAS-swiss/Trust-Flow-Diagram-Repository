 # Re-Identification at Age of Majority

Trust flow for re-identifying a customer who was originally onboarded as a minor
and has since turned 18.

> Not an official flow of the swiyu team or any other authority — published without
> warranty. Work in progress, for discussion purposes only.

## Contributors

This work was developed within the framework of **OpenBankingProject.ch** in the
working group **#6 E-ID + QES**.

The discussion and development involved representatives from approximately
**11 banks and around 20 participating organizations**.

- Roman Zoun (Swisscom)
