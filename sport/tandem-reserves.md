# Tandem reserves: manufacturer requirements

Swiss Skydive defers to the manufacturers: "the manufacturer's instructions
on packing, handling and use of the parachute are binding" and "the packing
cycle for the reserve is set by the manufacturer" (01-00d 01.02, 10.02).
Neither 01-00d nor 01-09d has a tandem-specific rigger rating or reserve rule
beyond the mandatory AAD on tandem rigs (01-00d 01.10). So the tandem rules a
repack credential and a drop zone have to respect are the manufacturers'.

Status: **draft**. Researched on 2026-09-25 from the manufacturers' published
manuals and bulletins; the documents and editions are named in each row.

For comparison, the US rule for tandems (14 CFR 105.45 b): the reserve is
packed by a certificated rigger (within 180 days, 105.43), the AAD is
approved by the tandem manufacturer, maintained and armed, and the reserve
meets TSO C23.

## Where manufacturers are stricter than 01-09d

| | 01-09d | Manufacturer | Consequence |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Major repairs on mains | Senior rigger may | UPT: master rigger on any part of a Sigma | For a UPT rig the rigger app requires `master-rigger` for major repairs |
| Major repairs on reserves | Master rigger may | Safran Atom, X-Skyspirit: factory only; Paradigm: damaged reserve canopy to the factory | The rigger app refuses a repair entry for these systems |
| Minor repairs | Senior rigger may (mains) | Paratec: master rigger or manufacturer only | Same |
| Packer | Senior rigger with rating F | Firebird (legacy): packers trained by Firebird; TNT: back-type rating | Record the manufacturer training in the rigger licence if Swiss Skydive wants to enforce it |
| Approval | TSO/JTSO only, or "a TSO of another organisation" meeting FAA-TSO (01-00d 10.05, 01-09d 01.04 c) | Atom Tandem: French regulations; Touch Tandem: QAC 121 | **Swiss decision needed** whether these count |

Swiss Skydive's rule that the manufacturer's instructions are binding
(01-00d 01.02) makes the stricter manufacturer rule apply in each case.

## What this adds to the credentials

**Reserve repack credential** — for a tandem rig, in addition to the
[general claims](./reserve-repack.md#the-repack-credential):

| Claim | Example | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `system_type` | `tandem` | Switches on the tandem checks |
| `approval_basis` | `TSO-C23F` | Distinguishes TSO from QAC/French approval |
| `reserve_uses`, `reserve_pack_count` | `2`, `17` | UPT, Safran, X-Skyspirit count uses and packs |
| `component_uses` | `{"rsl": 2, "skyhook": 2, "reserve_pilot_chute": 2}` | UPT limits them to 25 |
| `aad_mode` | `tandem` | Every manufacturer requires a tandem unit or tandem mode |
| `aad_approved_for_system` | `true` | Checked against the manufacturer's list at repack |
| `repack_cycle_days` | `365` | From the manufacturer; UPT states none, so the value written on the card applies |
| `major_repair` | `{"date": "…", "by": "master-rigger" or "factory"}` | Only when one was done |

The rigger app holds a per-system rule table (the one above) and refuses a
repack whose counters or AAD fall outside it. The drop zone then only checks
the credential, not the rules.

**Drop zone check before a tandem load**

- Repack credential valid, `system_type` tandem, `aad_mode` tandem.
- AAD switched on before boarding, reserve pin and closing loop through the
  pin window, RSL / Collins lanyard / Skyhook connected — physical checks on
  the rig, as the manuals require; no credential replaces them.
- Warning labels on harness and reserve (01-00d 10.05).
- Tandem licence valid and not suspended ([tandem pilot](./tandem-pilot.md)).
  Whether the drop zone also asks for the manufacturer's tandem rating for
  the system on the load is its own decision; 01-05 does not require it.
