# Food and beverage

Age check at the entrance of a nightclub, using the unlinkable presentation of the e-ID.

> Not an official flow of the swiyu team or any other authority — published without
> warranty. Work in progress, for discussion purposes only.

Each flow here is composed: its presentation step is the
[unlinkable presentation](../basic-flow/Unlinkable%20e-ID%20use/unlinkable-presentation.md)
block in `basic-flow/`, and the flow adds the requested claims, the legal basis
and the outcome. The [six-verifier walkthrough](../basic-flow/Unlinkable%20e-ID%20use/six-verifier-walkthrough.md)
shows them together.
